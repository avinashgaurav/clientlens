# Demo video script (45 to 60 seconds)

This is the highest-leverage asset for the launch and the one thing nobody else can produce for you: it needs a configured extension in a real Meet call. Everything else in the launch can be faked or written. This cannot.

**Format:** 1920x1080, screen recording, no talking head. Captions burned in (most PH viewers watch muted). No music, or something so quiet it is subliminal. Export under 15 MB if you also want it inline in the README as a GIF.

**Golden rule:** the viewer must see a *live call* within the first 4 seconds. Not a logo, not a title card, not your face. The product's entire claim is "during the call", so prove it immediately.

## Preparation

You need a believable call, which means a second participant and a scripted objection. Do not try to do this solo with an empty Meet room; a one-person call with no audio makes every agent look dead.

1. **Seed the KB properly.** Load 3 or 4 real-looking case studies with actual numbers in them, so the validator has something to cite and the tooltip in shot 4 shows a real quote. The sample-data toggle works, but hand-written entries look better on camera.
2. **Script the objection.** Have your counterpart say a clean, quotable line: "Honestly, this is more expensive than what we are using now." Clean audio in, clean coaching cue out.
3. **Do a dry run first** and check where the coaching cue actually fires. If it lags by 8 seconds, record the take anyway and cut the dead time; do not fake the cue.
4. **Use a fake prospect and fake company.** Never record a real prospect. Obvious, but worth writing down: this footage is going on the public internet.
5. **Zoom the browser to 110 to 125%.** The sidebar text is small, and PH plays video in a small frame. Unreadable text is the most common demo video failure.
6. **Hide anything real.** Bookmarks bar off, notifications off, clean Chrome profile, no other tabs with customer names.

## After recording

- Trim mercilessly. Any moment where nothing on screen changes is a moment a viewer leaves.
- Burn in the captions from the table above. Do not rely on PH's player.
- Export a short GIF of shots 3 and 4 only (about 10 seconds) for the top of the README. That single loop does more for the repo than the full video.
- Put the full video in the PH gallery as the **first** item, before any screenshot.
