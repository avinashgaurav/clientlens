Poke around

Every screen, in the order you meet them.

Real screenshots, not mockups. This is the whole loop, from loading your material to reading your week.

01

Load your material.

Paste case studies and battle cards, drop in files, point it at a URL or a git repo. Each entry is indexed and chunked, and every citation later resolves back to one of them.

  • Categories keep sources separated by purpose
  • PMMs can keep it current without bothering engineering
  • Nothing is generated from material you did not give it

02

Draft the pitch.

Pick an output format and the role you are selling to. A four-agent council does retrieval, personalisation, brand compliance and fact-checking in one pass.

  • Five formats, from slide cards to a dense exec one-pager
  • Buyer personas tuned for CFO, CTO, COO, VP Sales and more
  • Anything uncited is rejected before it reaches you

03

Take the calls.

Join a Google Meet and the sidebar starts listening. Afterwards each call lands here with its sentiment and how much of your agenda you actually covered.

  • Sortable by date, sentiment, coverage or outcome
  • One click pushes the summary into Zoho
  • Nothing to install on the prospect's side

04

Read the week.

Win rate, average sentiment and every objection you handled, without writing a note. The loop closes itself.

  • Agenda coverage and sentiment trended across each call
  • Objections with the source citation that backed the answer
  • Action items with an owner and a due date

During the call

Five agents, one transcript.

They run on the live transcript while you talk. The last one exists to stop you saying a number you cannot back.

Sentiment

Reads the prospect's tone as the call moves, so you know when to soften and when to push.

Agenda

Tracks what you have covered against the plan, and warns you when discovery has not happened.

Coach

Surfaces the next-best sentence the moment the prospect pushes back.

Objection

Answers "they just said X, what do I say?" with a two-agent council.

Fact-check

Audits every number against your knowledge base. Anything uncited is rejected before you see it.

From clone to first call

Three steps, your infrastructure.

Node 20+, Python 3.11+, and one LLM provider key. A Groq or Gemini free tier is enough to evaluate the whole thing.

git clone https://github.com/avinashgaurav/project-wingman.git
cd project-wingman

bash scripts/setup_env.sh          # writes backend/.env and extension/.env

cd backend && python3.11 -m venv venv && source venv/bin/activate
pip install -r requirements.txt
uvicorn main:app --reload --port 8000

cd ../extension && npm install && npm run dev

Then load extension/dist/ at chrome://extensions with Developer mode on. There is no sign-in step.
