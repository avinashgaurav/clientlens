The catch

Things you should hear from me, not from a bug report.

It is v1.0 and it is one person. Here is exactly where the edges are.

01

Setup is a developer job. Clone it, run a backend, bring a key. No Web Store listing yet.

02

There is no login. Built for one rep on their own machine. Keep that backend off the open internet.

03

Google Meet and Zoho only. Teams, HubSpot and Salesforce are on the list, not in the box.

04

Your LLM bill is yours. Gemini’s free tier covers a trial. There is nothing to cancel, ever.
