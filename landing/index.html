01 · Live meeting copilot

Five agents coaching you in real time.

The second you join a Google Meet, Wingman starts listening. Five specialised agents run on the rolling transcript — sentiment, agenda, coach, objection, and the validator that fact-checks every number you're about to say.

  • Sentiment Reads the prospect's tone every few seconds. Tells you when to soften, when to push.
  • Agenda Tracks topics covered vs. planned. Warns you 12 minutes in if you haven't done discovery.
  • Coach Surfaces the next-best-sentence the moment the prospect pushes back.
  • Objection Three-agent council answers "they just said X, what do I say?"
  • Validator Every number you cite is fact-checked against your knowledge base. No surprises.

02 · Pitch generation

Pitches grounded in your battle cards, never invented.

Pick the ICP — CFO, CTO, VP Sales, RevOps, Generic — paste the prospect's site or LinkedIn, and the council does the rest. Output is presentation, one-pager, detailed doc, or analysis-style writeup. Every numeric claim cites a source_id. Validator rejects anything unsourced before it ever reaches you.

  • CFO Numbers-first, hedged. Payback period, ROI breakdown, hidden costs.
  • CTO Architecture, security posture, integration surface, scaling characteristics.
  • VP Sales Win rates, ACV uplift, competitive displacement, case studies.
  • RevOps Workflow automation, attribution, integration hygiene.
03 · Post-call insights

Your week, finally legible.

Every call ends with a structured summary: agenda coverage, sentiment timeline, objections raised, action items, sourced quotes. One click pushes it to Zoho as a CRM note. Your Insights tab is the album-grid of every conversation you've had — scannable, sortable, replayable.

  • Sentiment Trended across the call. See exactly when it shifted, and why.
  • Agenda What got covered, what got skipped. Roll into next-touch agenda automatically.
  • Objections Each objection, the response quality, and the source citation that backed it.
  • Next steps Action items with owner and due date. CRM-ready in one click.

From install to first close

Three steps. Ten minutes. No tab switching.

Wingman runs entirely in your browser sidebar. No installs on the prospect side, no shared zoom plugin to sell-in, no IT ticket. You install once, sign in with Google, and you're coaching.

1

Install the extension

Drop the unpacked extension into Chrome (or grab it from the Web Store when we ship). Sign in with your Google workspace account. The sidebar opens.

2

Add your knowledge base

Upload case studies, battle cards, pricing decks, brand guides. Wingman chunks and embeds them locally with Gemini, and indexes into an in-browser vector store. Designers and PMMs can update without bothering eng.

3

Join your next Meet

The sidebar lights up. Five agents start coaching. The transponder overlays on Meet itself. You stop juggling tabs and start running better calls. Post-call, one click pushes the summary to Zoho.
