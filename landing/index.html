01 · Live meeting copilot

Five agents coaching you in real time.

The second you join a Google Meet, Wingman starts listening. Five specialised agents run on the rolling transcript — sentiment, agenda, coach, objection, and the validator that fact-checks every number against your knowledge base before it leaves your mouth.

  • Sentiment Reads the prospect's tone every few seconds. Tells you when to soften, when to push.
  • Agenda Tracks topics covered vs. planned. Warns you 12 minutes in if you haven't done discovery.
  • Coach Surfaces the next-best-sentence the moment the prospect pushes back — always grounded in your KB.
  • Objection Three-agent council answers: given exactly what the prospect just said, what do you say next?
  • Validator Every number you cite is checked against your KB. Unsourced claims are flagged before you say them.

02 · Pitch generation

Pitches grounded in your battle cards, never invented.

Pick the ICP — CFO, CTO, VP Sales, RevOps, Generic — paste the prospect's site or LinkedIn, and the council does the rest. Output is presentation, one-pager, detailed doc, or analysis-style writeup. Every numeric claim cites a source_id. Validator rejects anything unsourced before it ever reaches you.

  • CFO Numbers-first, hedged. Payback period, ROI breakdown, hidden costs.
  • CTO Architecture, security posture, integration surface, scaling characteristics.
  • VP Sales Win rates, ACV uplift, competitive displacement, case studies.
  • RevOps Workflow automation, attribution, integration hygiene.
03 · Post-call insights

Your week, finally legible.

Every call ends with a structured summary: agenda coverage, sentiment timeline, objections raised, action items, sourced quotes. One click syncs it to your CRM. Your Insights tab is a scannable, sortable repository of every conversation you've had.

  • Sentiment Trended across the call. See exactly when it shifted, and why.
  • Agenda What got covered, what got skipped. Roll into next-touch agenda automatically.
  • Objections Each objection, the response quality, and the source citation that backed it.
  • Next steps Action items with owner and due date. CRM-ready in one click.

From install to first close

Three steps. Ten minutes. No tab switching.

Wingman runs entirely in your browser sidebar. No installs on the prospect side, no shared recording plugin, no IT ticket. You install once, sign in with Google, and you're coaching.

1

Install the extension

Currently in open beta — install from the latest GitHub release. Chrome Web Store submission is in progress. Sign in with your Google Workspace account. The sidebar opens.

2

Add your knowledge base

Upload case studies, battle cards, pricing decks, brand guides. Wingman chunks and embeds them using your configured LLM provider — the same key you set in Step 1. Everything is indexed in-browser. No files leave your machine to Wingman's servers.

3

Join your next Meet

The sidebar lights up. Five agents start coaching. You stop juggling tabs and start running better calls. Post-call, one click syncs the summary to Zoho CRM. Salesforce and HubSpot sync are on the roadmap.
