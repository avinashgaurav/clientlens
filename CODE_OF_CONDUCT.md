# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is trying to help. Criticise code, not people.

## What that means here

Things that are welcome: direct technical disagreement, blunt reviews, saying a
design is wrong and explaining why, reporting that the setup broke on your
machine even if you suspect it is your fault.

Things that are not: personal attacks, harassment, sexual attention, insults
about someone's identity or experience level, publishing private information,
and persistently derailing threads after being asked to stop.

Being right does not license being cruel. A correct review delivered as an insult
is still a problem, and "I was just being honest" does not cover it.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any other project space,
plus cases where someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Email **220693974+avinashgaurav@users.noreply.github.com** with what happened and where. Reports go to the
one maintainer, who will keep the reporter's identity to themselves.

If the report is about the maintainer, and that is an awkward thing to raise with
the same person, say so directly and it will be handled as such.

## Enforcement

In rough order: a private word, a public correction, deleting or editing the
content, a temporary block, a permanent block. Which one depends on severity and
on whether it keeps happening. The maintainer decides, and will explain the
reasoning if asked.
