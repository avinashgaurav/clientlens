# Northwind Notes: Pricing and security FAQ (fictional sample KB)

## Plans

There are two plans. The Team plan is billed per seat per month. The
Enterprise plan adds SSO, audit logs, and a dedicated support contact.
A 14-day free trial is available on both plans.

## Data security

Notes are encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.2 or later.
Workspace owners can export all data as Markdown at any time.

## Audit logs

On the Enterprise plan, audit logs record sign-ins, permission changes, and
exports. Logs are retained for 365 days.
